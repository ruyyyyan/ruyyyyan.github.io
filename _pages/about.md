I am currently a Ph.D. student majoring in **Theoretical Physics** at **Soochow University**.

My research focuses on computational condensed matter and theoretical physics. During my master's studies, I primarily worked on **strongly correlated electron systems** and **superconductivity physics**. For my doctoral research, I focus on **ferroelectric physics**, employing **first-principles (ab initio) calculations** and **effective Hamiltonian methods**. Recently, I have developed a keen interest in exploring **chaotic and nonlinear dynamical behaviors in two-dimensional (2D) sliding ferroelectrics**.

---

## Research Interests

- **Ferroelectric Physics & 2D Materials**: Sliding ferroelectricity, polarization switching, inter-layer couplings, and topological / domain wall structures.
- **Nonlinear Dynamics & Chaos**: Chaotic dynamics and nonlinear response in low-dimensional ferroelectric and multistable systems.
- **First-Principles Calculations & Effective Hamiltonian**: Density functional theory (DFT), constructing effective Hamiltonian models, and large-scale atomistic simulations.
- **Strongly Correlated Electron Systems & Superconductivity**: Many-body physics, electronic correlations, and mechanism of unconventional superconductivity.
